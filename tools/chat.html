A 180-billion-parameter model on one gaming GPU.

Qwen3.8-Flash-Next runs here on this computer's GPU with Glyd's own engine and compression. Nothing you type leaves this computer.

Fast and precise

This is Glyd's Hummingbird level, its most compact. Fast mode keeps every expert in Glyd's smallest format, so more of them fit on the GPU. Precise mode adds the precision pack: more bits where they matter, and stays closer to the original model. In both, the engine guesses several words ahead with the model's own prediction head and checks them in one pass: faster answers, the same words.
